# 🍽️ Restaurant Data Analysis & Recommendation (Capstone Project)

This capstone project involves a comprehensive exploratory data analysis (EDA) on restaurant data from various countries and cities to extract insights on customer preferences, restaurant popularity, cuisines, and delivery trends. The project also builds a foundation for a restaurant recommender system.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To analyze restaurant-related data to:

- Understand global distribution and concentration of restaurants.
- Explore the impact of services like online delivery and table booking.
- Identify top cuisines and their locality-wise trends.
- Investigate user voting patterns and cost relationships.
- Discover key factors that influence restaurant ratings.

---

## 📁 Dataset

- `data.xlsx` – Contains restaurant-level data including city, cuisines, votes, costs, delivery options, etc.
- `Country-Code.xlsx` – Mapping of country codes to country names.

---

## 🔧 Technologies Used

- **Language**: Python 3.x
- **Libraries**:
  - `pandas`, `numpy` – Data manipulation
  - `matplotlib`, `seaborn` – Visualization
  - `scikit-learn` – Outlier handling
  - `statistics` – Statistical calculations

---

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis Highlights

- ✅ Country-wise and city-wise restaurant distribution
- ✅ Table booking vs. no booking restaurants (ratio + pie chart)
- ✅ Online delivery vs. no delivery availability (ratio + pie chart)
- ✅ Outlier detection and removal (Votes & Average Cost for Two)
- ✅ Most popular cuisines by locality and restaurant
- ✅ Heatmap correlation of features affecting restaurant ratings
- ✅ Identification of the most voted and best-rated restaurants

---

## 📈 Key Insights

- **New Delhi** has the highest number of restaurants.
- Restaurants that offer **online delivery** tend to receive more votes.
- Most restaurants serve **1–2 cuisines**, but chains like **Cafe Coffee Day** serve the most diverse menu.
- No single feature strongly influences ratings, but **votes, price range**, and **services** do contribute.
